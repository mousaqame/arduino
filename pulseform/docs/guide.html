Pulseform

An original rhythm platformer in Godot 4 — every asset generated in code.

Godot 4.4+ GDScript 240 Hz physics Synthesized audio Procedural neon

The idea

A one-button runner: a glowing cube auto-scrolls forward and you jump in time with the music to clear spikes and blocks, bounce off pads, and snap through rings. It borrows the feel of the genre while every pixel, note, and line of UI is original and made from scratch.

Why it's built entirely in code

Instead of hand-authored scenes and imported art, the whole game is constructed in GDScript. A one-node Main.tscn boots Main.gd, which builds every screen, object, and bit of UI at runtime. That keeps the project robust — there are almost no binary files to drift out of sync — and makes the "100% original assets" rule literal: the art is drawing code, the music is synthesis code.

Systems

  • EventBus — a single global signal hub; systems never reference each other directly, only the bus. That's what keeps player, camera, audio, and UI independently swappable.
  • Physics — a fixed 240 Hz tick makes motion frame-independent and deterministic. The ground is an infinite boundary and obstacles are boxes, so there's no tunneling. A crash is detected by lost forward progress, which keeps clean landings fair.
  • Audio — a 4-bar groove (kick, retriggered saw bass, lead arpeggio, hat) and all SFX are rendered into 16-bit PCM at load. A beat clock derived from the audio position drives the pulsing visuals.
  • Juice — procedural glow, spin + squash, a one-node motion trail and particle burst, trauma-based screen shake, and a death slow-mo.
  • Save — encrypted, versioned, and mirrored to a backup that's only refreshed once a good primary write is verified.

Run it

Open this folder in Godot 4.4+ (Import project.godot) and press F5.
