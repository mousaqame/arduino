            It does not matter which outer leg of the knob goes to 5V — swapping them just
            reverses which way it turns, and there's a Reverse button for that anyway.
          

          

            One small servo can run from the Arduino's own 5V. If you add more, or it stutters
            under load, give the servos their own 5V supply and join the grounds.
