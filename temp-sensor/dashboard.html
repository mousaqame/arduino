            A DHT11 is a rough instrument
            

              It reads in whole degrees and is only accurate to about ±2 °C, so don't
              expect it to agree exactly with another thermometer. It is good at showing
              which way things are moving, which is usually what you actually want.
            

          

          

            Breathe gently on the sensor — the humidity should jump within a few seconds,
            and the temperature a little after. That's the quickest way to prove it works.
