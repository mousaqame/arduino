Parking Sensor

connecting…

This little machine measures how far away something is and beeps faster as you get closer — like the parking sensor in a car. Follow the four steps and it will be working in a few minutes.

1

Plug the Arduino into your computer

Use the USB cable. The little green light on the board should come on.

Looking for your board…
2

Connect the parts

Four things plug into the Arduino. Each coloured line below is one connection — match the pin number on the board to the part.

PartArduino pinWhat it does
Little screenA4, A5Shows the distance in numbers
Distance sensor9, 10Sends out a click and times the echo
LED light6Lights up when something is close
Buzzer7Beeps faster the closer you get

Not sure the LED is in the right way round? Pick the Blink test in step 3 — if the light flashes, it's wired correctly.

3

Send the code to the Arduino

The Arduino is empty until you give it instructions. Pick what you want it to do, then press the button. It takes about ten seconds.
